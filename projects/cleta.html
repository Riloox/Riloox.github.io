Cleta

Campus bike share for UTEC Fray Bentos (3 km outside town). Final degree project with Gabriel De León and Mikaella Mateos.

What it is

Replaces UTEC's manual bike lending list. Students sign in with their university Google account, pick a bike on a map, scan its QR code, unlock the ring lock over Bluetooth, and upload a return photo.

Built in 16 weeks across three parts: a React Native Android app, a React and TypeScript admin panel, and an Express plus PostgreSQL API on GCP. I wrote 426 backend commits: API, Prisma schema, revocable JWTs, roles, and the lock service.

Loans enforce one active bike per user, a 120-minute weekly quota reset Sunday at midnight, and a 20-minute warning notification.
