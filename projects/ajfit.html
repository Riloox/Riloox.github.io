AJFit

Training app for a personal trainer and his clients. Private repo, paying client.

What it is

Built with React, TypeScript, Hono, Cloudflare Workers, and D1. Clients log sessions, check per-exercise progress and weekly muscle volume, send feedback, and get meal ideas from ingredients at home. The trainer sees one list sorted by who needs attention: unanswered feedback first, then inactive clients.

Hard parts

  • Coach triage: sorting clients by reply status and inactivity took more iterations than the rest of the app.
  • Strict exercise catalog: free-text entries broke progress charts, so variations are locked to a fixed catalog.
  • One-handed mobile logging: redesigned flows so clients can log sets between exercises on a phone.
  • Low-ops hosting: per-client data isolation, coach-run invites, and near-zero hosting cost on Workers and D1.
