Ene

Spanish tutor for Korean speakers. Local Gemma 4 E2B fine-tune plus an invite-only Cloudflare Worker app. Written Eñe in Spanish.

What it is

Write a Spanish sentence and ask in Korean if it is right. Ene rewrites it, names the change, explains the rule in Korean, and gives one exercise.

The demo runs on Cloudflare Workers and KV with Web Speech input and invite codes (email for one). The model is a Gemma 4 E2B QLoRA fine-tune trained on a 12 GB laptop GPU and served via Ollama.

Results and trade-offs

  • Scores: 177/208 vs 123/208 stock on a frozen 208-item set, mostly by leaving correct sentences alone; later round 181/208 vs 158 stock under a blind 26B judge.
  • What did not work: GRPO added +2 items (noise) because the reward could not grade Korean explanations.
  • Limits: text-only (llama.cpp drops vision and audio encoders); Korean explanations are judge-checked awaiting native calibration.

Full details in the training write-up.
